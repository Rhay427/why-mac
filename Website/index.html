Why? brings supported macOS signals together and presents the strongest explanation first.

Open it from the menu bar, ask for a check, and get a conclusion in about five seconds. Nothing runs continuously in the background.

Find what is keeping your processor busy.

Why? looks for sustained activity across two observations, then identifies the strongest qualifying process or application.

Connect battery use to active work.

While your Mac is on battery, Why? can explain a likely processor-related contributor without inventing drain rates or battery-life predictions.

Know what is delaying automatic sleep.

Supported macOS power assertions reveal who is preventing automatic idle sleep and why—without claiming that closing the lid or manual sleep is blocked.

See when memory pressure needs attention.

Why? uses macOS memory-pressure signals to explain a real constraint instead of treating low free memory as a problem by itself.
